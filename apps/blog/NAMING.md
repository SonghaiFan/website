# Public writing names

- **博客 / Blog** names the public website and its single landing page.
- **随笔 / Note** is every ordinary public article in `Blog/` or `Blog/en/`, including a dated filename or an article developed from a private daily note. Label its sidebar **全部随笔 / All notes**.
- **项目 / Project** is a public page in `Blog/projects/` or `Blog/en/projects/`.
- **Daily/** holds private daily writing. It is not a public blog type. Keep its dated file in place when developing a separate public Note.
- When a public article has no date, display **未标日期 / Undated** in the date slot. Keep its title unchanged.

Only `Blog/` is published. This vocabulary does not rename vault folders or require metadata on old posts.
